Our Story

Sip & Stay started as a small corner café with one big dream — to create a space where people slow down, connect, and enjoy really good coffee. We source our beans ethically from small farms, and bake everything fresh in-house every single morning.

Whether you're here for a quick espresso or a slow afternoon with a book, you're always welcome to stay a while.
